## Records taken from public sources

Titles, author names, journal names, identifiers, trial records, grant records,
dataset descriptions and standard codes come from the public sources below.
This project claims no rights in them. They keep the terms of their source.
Check a source's own terms before redistributing its records in bulk.

- PubMed and MeSH, U.S. National Library of Medicine
- Europe PMC
- OpenAlex
- Semantic Scholar
- ClinicalTrials.gov
- NIH RePORTER
- NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus and BioProject
- Cellosaurus
- NCI Thesaurus, OncoTree, MONDO, the Disease Ontology and Wikidata

## Not included

Abstracts and the full text of papers are not in this repository. They belong
to their publishers and authors.

## No warranty, and not medical advice

The data is provided as it is. Much of it was extracted by a model and has not
been checked by a clinician. It is a guide to the published research, not
advice about anyone's care.
